Glitch engine

Pitch shifts are tape-style: higher notes play faster, lower notes slower. Speed jumps make random steps half or double length.

FX rack applied per step, so every hit can sound different

Filter

A resonant filter on every hit. Jitter moves the cutoff per step; Sweep makes each hit zap down from bright to the cutoff.

Bitcrusher

Drops bit depth and sample rate for that broken-sampler grit. Chance sets how many steps get crushed.

Motion

Glides bend a hit's pitch up or down while it plays, and some become tape stops. Pan throws hits around the stereo field.
