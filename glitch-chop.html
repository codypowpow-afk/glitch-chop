Chop

Slices on a musical grid. Set the sample's tempo and meter, or let it fit the BPM to the file length.

Cuts wherever the volume jumps: syllables, consonants, breaths.

Tracks the pitch of the voice and cuts each time the sung note changes. This usually makes the best melodies.

Splits the whole sample into equal slices.

Melody engine

Every chop is tuned from the note it already sings to the note the melody needs. Pitch shifts are tape-style, so higher notes also play faster.

Sound rack Sound, Filter and pan change live · the rest apply on the next roll

Sustain

Each chop gets a seamless loop, so notes hold for as long as the melody asks.

Layers

Harmony stacks a second voice on held notes. Choir holds the chords underneath. Beat adds a simple drum kit in the groove.

Glitch

Chaos breaks the rules: wrong chops, wrong notes, detuning. Turn it up to get the old madness back.

Filter

A resonant filter on every hit. Jitter moves the cutoff per note; Sweep makes each hit zap from bright down to the cutoff.

Bitcrusher

Drops bit depth and sample rate for that broken-sampler grit. Chance sets how many notes get crushed.

Motion

Slides scoop into a note from the one before, like a singer. Bends and tape stops warp a note as it plays. Pan spreads hits across the stereo field.
